# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc].

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The full text and translations are maintained upstream at
`contributor-covenant.org`. Do not re-license or fork the text in this
repository; link to the canonical source instead.

## Scope

The Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces: the source
repositories under `github.com/sovantica/engrava`, the issue tracker,
pull requests, discussions, the release notes, and any official
communication channel that represents the project (for example an
engrava account on a social platform, or the engrava.ai website).

It also applies to individuals representing the project in public —
speaking at a conference about engrava, posting as a maintainer, or
otherwise acting on the project's behalf.

## Reporting

To report conduct concerns, email **conduct@engrava.ai**.

Include:

- What happened, when, and where (link to the thread, PR, or venue).
- The accounts or identities involved, as far as you know them.
- Any relevant context the responder would not be able to reconstruct
  from the public record.

Reports are read by the project maintainers only. We will acknowledge
within three business days. If the concern is about a maintainer, you
can escalate to **conduct@sovantica.ai** (the studio that stewards this
project) to reach someone outside the maintainer set.

Do not post conduct reports as public issues.

## Enforcement

Responses follow the four-tier ladder described in the Contributor
Covenant v2.1: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. The
responding maintainer selects a tier proportionate to the incident, the
pattern of prior behavior if any, and the impact on the people
involved. Decisions are documented privately in the maintainers'
records; public summaries are published only when the tier is a
temporary or permanent ban, and only with enough detail to explain the
action without re-exposing the reporter.
